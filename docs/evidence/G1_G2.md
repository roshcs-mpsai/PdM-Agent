**Verdict.** The false-alarm budget holds (0.36 per week against 2, upper bound 0.76). Every reported failure is alarmed, but only 1 of 4 before its report: F1 -0.80 h, F2 -0.27 h, F3 -0.32 h, F4 +13.73 h. A negative lead means the incident opened after the report began. G1's budget is met; useful warning is shown for F4. The four labels are day-granular reports of detection, so these leads are measured against when the failure was noticed, not its onset; the expert log's 21 failures, which the detector also alarms on, are the larger test (the detector-selection review, Section 8).
